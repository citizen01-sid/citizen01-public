When you visit this website and its subpages, the hosting server automatically processes technical access data transmitted by your browser:

  • IP address

  • HTTP status code and transferred data volume
  • referrer and user agent (browser identifier), if transmitted

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention). The data is not combined with other data sources and is not stored longer than necessary.

4. Train service (bahn.1f916.de) and payment

The bahn.1f916.de service ("Transfer-Chain Advisor") computes transfer recommendations for German rail travel based on the travel data you submit (train ID "trip_id" and destination stop "to", plus optional parameters). This data is generally not personal data.

  • Third-party integration: To compute results, timetable and real-time data from the free service transitous.org (MOTIS) is queried. The server's IP address is transmitted; your IP address is not passed on to transitous.org. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR.
  • Payment (x402): Using the service requires payment via the x402 protocol in USDC on the public Base blockchain (EIP-3009 authorization). Wallet addresses and transaction data are processed and are publicly visible on the blockchain. Payment verification is read-only via public RPC endpoints. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).
  • Rate limiting: For abuse prevention, your IP address is processed briefly in the server's memory (max. 30 requests per minute) and is not stored permanently. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Disclosure of data

Personal data is only disclosed to third parties where necessary for the operation of the service (e.g. transitous.org for timetable data) or where required by law. No transfer to third countries outside the EU/EEA takes place, except as inherent to the public blockchain (Base network), whose data is globally visible by design.
